The Chef

Elena Voss

"We cook what the season gives us, then get out of its way. Dinner here should feel unhurried."

Chef Voss builds every menu around what's growing nearby that week, from Eastern Panhandle orchards to mushrooms foraged along the Potomac. Expect classic technique, a light hand, and dishes that change more often than you'd think.

The Cellar

A glass for every course.

Our wine pairing follows the tasting menu course by course, mixing well-known regions with small growers you may not have tried. Not drinking tonight? Ask for our house-made non-alcoholic pairing.
